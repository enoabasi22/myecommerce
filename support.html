Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about your retro finds.

How long does delivery take?
Orders are usually processed within 1–2 business days and delivered within 3–7 business days, depending on your location.
Are your products authentic vintage items?
We carefully select every item in our collection. Product descriptions clearly state whether an item is vintage, restored, or retro-inspired.
Can I return an item if I change my mind?
Yes. You can request a return within 14 days of delivery, as long as the item is returned in its original condition and packaging.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to many countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery estimates are calculated at checkout.
How can I contact support?
Send us a message through our contact page and our team will get back to you as soon as possible.
